# Copyright and licensing notes

Copyright generally exists automatically when original code, text, and graphics are fixed in a file. For a student project, keep a clear authorship record with dates, contributors, and source citations.

Before public release, choose one of these paths:

1. All rights reserved: do not add an open-source license. Add a copyright notice such as `Copyright (c) 2026 [Student Name]. All rights reserved.`
2. Open source: add a standard license such as MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-3-Clause, or GPL after understanding what it permits.
3. School/organization project: verify ownership and publication rules with the school or program.

Do not copy proprietary datasets, maps, logos, articles, or model weights unless the license permits it.
